# Mergington High School Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

At Mergington High, we take the security of our Extra-Curricular Activities website seriously, especially since it contains student information.

If you discover a security vulnerability:

1. Do not create a public GitHub issue or disclose the vulnerability.
2. Open the repository's Security tab.
3. Click "Report a vulnerability".
4. Describe the vulnerability, steps to reproduce it, and its potential impact.
5. Email the IT Club faculty advisor at techsupport@mergingtonhigh.example.edu to inform them that a report was submitted. Do not include vulnerability details.

## Response Timeline

- Reports will be acknowledged within 2 school days.
- An initial assessment will be provided within 5 school days.
- Critical vulnerabilities affecting student data will be addressed immediately.
- A private fork may be created to develop and review the fix.

## Thank You

Thank you for helping protect our school's digital resources through responsible vulnerability disclosure.
